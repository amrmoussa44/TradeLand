Export and import

Two programs, one standard of quality.

Our range runs across two programs. The export program covers cool-climate orchard fruit and berries. The import program brings sun-grown citrus, tropical fruit, grapes and field vegetables. Together they keep produce moving to your shelves across the seasons.

Export

Orchard fruit and berries

Cool-climate apples in a range of high-color varieties, plus plump summer blueberries. Fruit is stored and graded to hold condition well beyond harvest.

How we work

From the field to your door, in four steps.

1

Source

We select fruit and vegetables from vetted growers at origin, matched to your quality and volume needs.

2

Grade and pack

Produce is graded, sized and packed to your specification, with labeling ready for your market.

3

Cold-chain logistics

Cooled and shipped by sea or air, with the cold chain maintained the whole way through.

4

Delivered

Export documentation is handled and produce arrives at your port or door, ready to move.
